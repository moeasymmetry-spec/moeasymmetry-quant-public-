# Why We Open-Source the Foundation

> *"Anyone can compute RS Rating. What they can't replicate is a daily integrated pipeline plus 36 years of cross-market validated backtests plus a live audit trail."*

## The Thai trading content landscape

Most Thai stock trading services are opaque "trust me" guru models:
- "I made 30% last month — subscribe to my picks"
- No published track record
- No verifiable backtests
- No methodology disclosure
- Marketing built on personality, not process

When the picks fail (as they inevitably do), subscribers blame themselves and resubscribe to the next guru. The cycle repeats.

We chose the opposite path.

## What we publish (free, forever)

- **The RS Rating formula** — public since 1988 (O'Neil). Not a secret.
- **The IBD market state machine** — also public.
- **Mike Webster's Power Trend** — public (deepvue.com, IBD videos).
- **Backtest source code** — anyone can reproduce our results.
- **Trade-by-trade CSVs** — every signal we've ever published.
- **Out-of-sample validation methodology** — not just summary stats.

## What we don't publish (gated behind subscription)

- **Today's integrated alpha picks** — daily computed output
- **Real-time alerts** — when our scanner fires
- **The full System DI/U/V/W rules synthesized** — the assembly is the work
- **Custom universe filtering tools**

## Why this works

1. **Trust** — when you publish the math, the WHO + WHEN of execution becomes the value
2. **SEO** — open-source projects get indexed globally; brings free organic traffic
3. **Defensive disclosure** — no one can claim we stole something we already published
4. **Recruits contributors** — other quants improve the open code, benefiting everyone
5. **Eliminates "guru risk"** — our credibility doesn't depend on opaque promises

## What you can do with this code

- **Compute RS Rating** for any market with daily OHLCV data
- **Detect IBD market state** for any major index
- **Run a simple Turtle 20-day breakout backtest**
- **Verify our daily numbers** against your own calculation
- **Build your own system** on top of this foundation

## What you can't do (without the paid platform)

- See **today's alpha picks** with entry/stop/sizing pre-calculated
- Receive **daily signal alerts** on our schedule
- Access the **full System W ruleset** (the council-validated synthesis)
- Use our **integrated scanner UI**

## Our bet

We bet that openness will outcompete opacity over a 5-10 year horizon. Customers who understand HOW our system works become better customers, longer customers, and advocates. Customers who blindly follow gurus churn.

If you're reading this and you're a fellow quant: please use this code, improve it, send PRs. The community of transparent traders is small but growing. We'd rather be small and honest than big and opaque.
